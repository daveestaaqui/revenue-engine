# Attorney Practice-Group Outreach

## Publication Gate

The template below is conditional on verified product capabilities. Before sending, confirm:

1. **Provenance:** Recipients can inspect the referenced court records or source documents.
2. **Lien screening:** Screening scope and exclusion logic are documented. “Elimination” means filtering records from a qualified view—not extinguishing liens or guaranteeing clean priority.
3. **Deadlines:** Calculations identify supported jurisdictions, governing authorities, triggering events, and assumptions. FRCP support is claimed only where applicable and actually implemented.
4. **Intake export:** Clio and Filevine CSV formats have been tested against the recipient’s applicable import workflow. A one-click download is not necessarily a one-click import.
5. **Trial:** The signup URL, terms, pricing disclosures, and available sample data are accurate.

Do not send unsupported feature claims with a disclaimer attached. Remove or narrow the claim.

## Sending and Conversion Rules

- Target firms with relevant practices in jurisdictions the product actually supports.
- Personalize using public professional information; avoid sensitive claimant details.
- Use a genuine sender identity and accurate title. Never imply the sender is counsel or a fellow attorney unless true.
- Send one relevant follow-up, then stop absent engagement.
- Maintain suppression lists and comply with applicable commercial-email and privacy requirements.
- Keep the CTA entirely self-serve: no calendar links, callback offers, or mandatory sales qualification.
- Disclose trial duration, payment requirements, and conversion terms before signup.
- Link directly to a focused trial experience—not a generic homepage.
- Preserve the same terminology across email, landing page, onboarding, and product UI.

## Recommended Trial Activation Path

1. Choose a supported jurisdiction and proceeding type.
2. Open a source-linked record.
3. Inspect lien-screening findings and unresolved issues.
4. Review any calculated deadline and its governing authority.
5. Download a tested intake CSV.

**Primary conversion event:** A qualified attorney completes a source-record review and usable export—not merely an email open or sign
