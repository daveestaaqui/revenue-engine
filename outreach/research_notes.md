# Surplus Fund Recovery Attorneys - Research Notes

## Overview
I conducted targeted research to identify real, verified law firms and attorneys specializing in the asset recovery niche, specifically focusing on tax deed surplus funds, excess proceeds claims, and foreclosure overages. The contacts are compiled in `verified_attorney_targets.csv`.

## State-by-State Findings
The research heavily targeted states with high volumes of tax deed and foreclosure sales, which consequently have active surplus recovery markets.

### Florida (FL)
- **Market Characteristics:** Highly active market due to the high volume of tax deed sales. Florida Statutes Chapter 45 allows former property owners and lienholders to claim surplus funds.
- **Attorney Profiles:** Many solo practitioners and small boutique firms operate on a contingency fee basis. The style tends to be either highly consultative (guiding clients through the process) or aggressive (fighting other lienholders for priority). 
- **Key Target Types:** Law firms specializing in Foreclosure Defense and Real Estate Litigation often pivot to or include surplus fund recovery.

### Texas (TX)
- **Market Characteristics:** Texas has a strict two-year statute of limitations to claim excess proceeds following a tax sale (Texas Tax Code). The funds are held by the county District Clerk.
- **Attorney Profiles:** Texas attorneys in this space are often detail-oriented, as the process involves proving heirship and navigating strict statutory requirements. 
- **Key Target Types:** Solo practitioners focusing on Real Estate, Probate, and Tax Law.

### California (CA) & Other States (GA, NC, TN, NY, NJ)
- **Market Characteristics:** California refers to these as "excess proceeds" from tax default sales. Georgia has an active "excess funds" market following tax sales.
- **Attorney Profiles:** In CA and NY, firms tend to be slightly larger or charge higher contingency fees due to complex state laws. In states like GA and NC, attorneys emphasize local county court connections.

## Common Traits of Ideal Targets (Solo / Small Firms)
1. **Web Presence:** Many have slightly outdated or basic websites, but explicitly mention "Surplus Funds", "Excess Proceeds", or "Foreclosure Overages" on their practice areas page.
2. **Contact Methods:** They heavily rely on form submissions or direct email (`info@` or the attorney's first name).
3. **Value Proposition:** Almost all of them advertise "No Upfront Fees" and work on contingency.
4. **Pain Points:** They spend a lot of time on skip tracing (finding the heirs/former owners) and dealing with county clerks. This makes them great targets for B2B services (software, skip tracing, CRM, marketing).

## Recommendations for Outreach
- **Personalization:** Mention the specific term used in their state (e.g., use "Excess Proceeds" for Texas and California, but "Surplus Funds" for Florida and Georgia).
- **Acknowledge the Grind:** Acknowledge that skip tracing and county clerk bureaucracy are major bottlenecks for them.
- **Highlight Contingency:** Since they work on contingency, frame your offering as a way to increase their throughput and win rate without adding significant overhead.
