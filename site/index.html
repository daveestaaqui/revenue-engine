Frequently Asked Questions

Key legal and delivery details on our public records feeds.

How does daily data delivery work?

Subscribers receive standardized CSV, XLSX, and JSON files every morning at 7:00 AM EST. Feeds include verified case docket numbers, surplus balances, property addresses, and calculated statutory deadlines ready for CRM import.

How are Florida, Texas & Georgia statutory claims handled?

Feeds track specific statutory rules: Florida (Fla. Stat. § 197.582, 120-day notice window, 20% cap benchmark), Texas (Tex. Tax Code § 34.04, 2-year limitation, 25% cap benchmark), and Georgia (O.C.G.A. § 48-4-5, 5-year claim window).

What makes Surplus Docket different from raw PDF lists?

Raw county lists are 70–80% encumbered by bank and senior mortgage liens. Surplus Docket algorithmically scrubs institutional liens so your team only targets pure individual owner and estate heir equity.

Can I cancel anytime? What is your policy?

Yes. Subscriptions include 1-click self-service cancellation anytime via our Stripe Customer Portal with no contracts. Annual plans include a 14-day evaluation guarantee.
