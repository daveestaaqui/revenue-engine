DeKalb County Recovery Procedure & Verification

When a tax deed auction or sheriff's tax sale closes above the upset bid in DeKalb County, the clerk deposits excess funds into the statutory court registry. Under O.C.G.A. § 48-4-5, junior lienholders have initial notice priority, after which remaining funds belong to the record titleholder or heirs.

Surplus Docket's automated crawler monitors DeKalb County filings daily, excludes institutional and bank-owned records, flags estates for heir search, and formats an actionable CSV and Excel feed ready for practitioner due diligence (lien examination remains firm responsibility).
