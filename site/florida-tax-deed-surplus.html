Florida Tax Deed Surplus Claims & Overbid Intelligence

Comprehensive statutory framework, claim filing timelines, and automated daily data feed for Florida tax deed excess proceeds.

Statutory Authority: Fla. Stat. § 197.582

Under Florida law, when real property is sold at a tax deed auction for an amount exceeding the statutory opening bid (statutory taxes, interest, and clerk costs), the excess funds constitute surplus proceeds.

The Clerk of the Circuit Court holds these funds in the court registry and issues a Notice of Surplus to all persons who held an interest in the property as recorded in the property records.

Claimant Priority & Statutory Timelines

Florida law establishes a strict priority scheme for distributing tax deed surplus proceeds:

  1. Government Liens: Municipal liens and special assessments of record.
  2. Senior Lienholders: First mortgage holders, recorded judgments according to recording priority.
  3. Junior Lienholders: Second mortgages, HOA/COA liens, subordinate judgment creditors.
  4. Titleholder of Record: The former owner(s) or their legal heirs as of the date the tax deed was executed.
120-Day Window: Claimants must submit notarized claims within 120 days of the date of the clerk's surplus notice. The feed updates daily at 7:00 AM EST with newly generated notices.
