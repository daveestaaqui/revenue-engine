15-Minute Cold Tap-Water Bench Screen (No Cooker, No DI Water, No Probe Sonicator)

Evaluate SLIP-NET™ v8.0 on your own coating bench in 15 minutes using ambient municipal tap water (50–200 ppm CaCO3), one 250 mL beaker, one #12 Meyer rod pass (~8 g/m² dry), and a 30 s bake at 125°C.

3 Common Bench Pitfalls to Avoid on Day 1: (1) Use Micronized Food-Grade TCP (d50 ≤ 1.8 µm, e.g. Innophos VersaCal® MP, Budenheim CAFOS® M) or milled DCPD (Brushite), NEVER coarse 15–150 µm tableting TCP (Tri-Tab®). (2) Use Cold-Water-Soluble (CWS) Pregelatinized Thin Starch (Roquette PREGEFLO®, Cargill C*iCoat™, GPC MALTRIN® / INSTANT PURE-COTE® B792) in Track B—raw granular thin-boiling starch settles insoluble at 22°C. (3) Keep Ca:P ≥ 1.00 (DAP 1.8–3.2 wt%) whenever using Na-Alginate so soluble Na2HPO4 never unzips Cobb60!

Track A: 5-Ingredient Zero-Cook Bench MVP (MVP-5, 25% Solids)

  1. Dissolve 0.50 g food-grade DAP (2.0 wt% dry; or 0.58 g / 2.3% for Option A2 hybrid) in 72.88 g ambient tap water (check pH ~8.7–8.8).
  2. Option A1 (Zero Na+, Best): Add 8.00 g ULV NH4-Alg (32%) — OR disperse 8.00 g Alginic Acid §184.1011 (zero clumping!) + 3.0 mL 28% NH4OH §184.1139 for 3 min. Option A2 Hybrid: 4.0 g ULV Na-Alg + 4.0 g Alginic/NH4OH.
  3. Mix in 12.98 g platy kaolin (51.9%, PVC 42% < CPVC), 1.40 g micronized TCP or DCPD (5.6%, Ca:P = 1.06), and 4.25 g of 50% Ca-Stearate disp. (8.5% dry).
  4. Pull ONE #12 Meyer rod pass (~7.8 g/m²) on smooth MG/SBS board and cure 30 s @ 125°C (pred: 749–762 cP, Cobb60 12.8, Kit 12.0, Crease Kit 10.9–11.0).

Track B: Cold-Mix Mill Grade (MILL-6, 30% Solids, 1.48–2.20 ¢/m²)

  • 48.8–51.3% Platy Kaolin (Imerys Barrisurf AR ≥ 45).
  • 19.5–20.0% CWS Pregelatinized Thin Starch (21 CFR §182.90, e.g. PREGEFLO® / C*iCoat™ / MALTRIN®) — dissolves 100% cold at 22°C!
  • M3 (NH4-Alg, 2.20 ¢/m²): 15.5% NH4-Alg + 5.2% Micron TCP + 2.0% DAP + 6.5% CaSt (738 cP, 90°C Fries 74.2%, 99.96% MC).
  • M4 (Commodity Na-Alg, 1.48 ¢/m²): 14.0% ULV Na-Alg + 7.5% Micron TCP + 3.2% DAP + 6.5% CaSt (Ca:P = 1.00, 633 cP, Cobb60 16.0, Crease Kit 10.1, 99.92% MC).
