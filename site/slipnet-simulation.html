A. Architecture Comparison Across 5 Industry Gate Criteria

Illustrative Target Comparison: Conventional barriers force trade-offs between fluorochemical bans, plastic repulper rejects, or carbonate CO₂ blistering. SLIP-NET™ targets all five gates simultaneously.

B. Failure Traps Eliminated by Phosphate-Triggered Architecture

Architectural Differentiation: Replacing carbonate crosslinkers and synthetic wet-strength binders with a latent phosphate trigger avoids gas blistering while preserving reversible ionic repulpability.

1. Hard-Water Gelation & Open-Pan Skinning

Conventional Trap: Ambient tap-water hardness (Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺) and atmospheric CO₂ shifts trigger premature alginate skinning in open coating pans.
SLIP-NET™ Approach: Food-grade phosphate buffering suppresses free Ca²⁺ in the wet pot without unstable polyphosphate hydrolysis traps.

2. Thermal CO₂ Blistering & Crater Pinholes

Conventional Trap: Carbonate crosslinkers evolve CO₂ gas during acidic thermal drying, creating micro-volcanoes and oil-wicking craters.
SLIP-NET™ Approach: Phosphate-based latent mineral dissolution supplies Ca²⁺ ions cleanly upon drying with zero gas evolution.

3. 180° Score-Line Crease Cracking

Conventional Trap: Over-pigmented mineral barriers above CPVC crack along folded carton scores, collapsing grease Kit resistance.
SLIP-NET™ Approach: Platy pigment geometry below CPVC combined with an aqueous slip dispersion maintains ductile fold integrity.

4. Mill Repulper Rejects & Microplastics

Conventional Trap: PE extrusion and synthetic latexes leave un-repulpable plastic flakes and covalent wet-strength rejects.
SLIP-NET™ Approach: Purely ionic crosslinking is designed to unzip in both neutral-pH recycling and alkaline mill-broke systems.

Non-Confidential Evaluation Process & Regulatory Class Overview

SLIP-NET™ is engineered for rapid laboratory and pilot screening using ambient municipal tap water, standard drawdown or metering-rod coaters, and conventional forced-air drying—with no thermal starch cooking or solvent recovery.

Confidential Formulation & Bench Protocol Access: Exact batch recipes, mixing sequence, and 15-minute bench screening matrix are provided immediately upon execution of a zero-cost Mutual NDA or your organization's standard CDA.

Non-Confidential Ingredient Classes

  • Platy natural mineral pigment — non-nano platelet geometry (>150 nm) engineered for barrier tortuosity and sub-CPVC fold ductility.
  • Low-viscosity seaweed biopolymer — unmodified natural polysaccharide forming the percolating, ionically reversible barrier network.
  • Food-grade phosphate latent trigger — non-gas-evolving calcium mineral and phosphate buffer system providing cold pot stability and thermal cure.
  • Aqueous wax / fatty-acid dispersion — surface hydrophobicity, Cobb60 water resistance, and 180° score-line slip.
  • Optional food-grade biopolymer co-binder & polyol plasticizer — cold-water-compatible modifiers for cost optimization and extreme fold flexibility.
