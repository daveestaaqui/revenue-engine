B. Failure Traps Eliminated by SLIP-NET™ V3 Architecture

Architectural Differentiation: Combining a two-zone humidified pre-cure with nonionic plant-based carnauba wax (Tm ~ 80–88°C) avoids dry-oven flash-skinning, CO₂ pinholes, and 52°C repulper stickies.

1. Hard-Water Gelation & Pot Instability

Conventional Trap: Ambient tap-water hardness (Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺) and ionic emulsions trigger premature gelation and open-pan skinning.
SLIP-NET™ V3 Approach: Food-grade calcium phosphate & volatile-base latent trigger suppresses free Ca²⁺ while nonionic plant-based carnauba wax dispersion remains electrolyte-stable.

2. Dry-Oven Flash-Skinning & CO₂ Blisters

Conventional Trap: Dry lab-oven flash-drying forms a surface skin that halts ionic transport, while carbonates evolve CO₂ craters.
SLIP-NET™ V3 Approach: Two-Zone Humidified Pre-Cure matches paper-mill dryer-hood humidity for clean zero-CO₂ ionic crosslinking prior to dry carnauba melt-coalescence.

3. 180° Score-Line Crease Cracking

Conventional Trap: Over-pigmented mineral barriers above CPVC crack along folded carton scores, collapsing grease Kit resistance.
SLIP-NET™ V3 Approach: Platy natural mineral below CPVC, bio-based polyol plasticizer, and melt-coalesced carnauba wax (Single-Pass or Two-Pass Pure-Wax Topcoat) maintain ductile fold integrity.

4. Repulper Stickies, Latex & Microplastics

Conventional Trap: PE, synthetic/natural latexes, and low-melting waxes smear into tacky rejects during warm repulping.
SLIP-NET™ V3 Approach: 100% latex-free ionic network unzips cleanly while high-melting carnauba wax (Tm ~ 80–88°C) remains a non-tacky crystalline solid at 52°C (52°C < 84°C) in WMU OCC-E & TAPPI T 275.

Non-Confidential V3 Ingredient Classes

  • Platy natural mineral — non-nano platelet geometry (>150 nm) engineered for barrier tortuosity and sub-CPVC fold ductility.
  • Ultra-low-viscosity marine biopolymer — unmodified natural seaweed polysaccharide forming the percolating, ionically reversible barrier network.
  • Food-grade calcium phosphate & volatile-base latent trigger — non-gas-evolving mineral and latent buffer system providing ambient pot stability and clean two-zone thermal cure.
  • Nonionic plant-based carnauba wax dispersion (Tm ~ 80–88°C) — provides (a) electrolyte stability in the wet pot, (b) clean dry thermal melt-coalescence, and (c) crystalline solid-state non-stickiness during 52°C neutral-pH WMU OCC-E repulping (52°C < 84°C).
