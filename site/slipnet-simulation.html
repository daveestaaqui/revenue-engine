Track A: Simplest 5-Ingredient Zero-Cook Bench MVP (MVP-5, 25% Solids)

  1. Pour 75.0 g ambient municipal tap water (subtract water inside CaSt dispersion) into one beaker; dissolve 1.63 g food-grade DAP (6.5 wt% dry for Na-Alg, or 1.20 g / 4.8% for NH4-Alg; verify pH ~8.7–8.8).
  2. Stir in 8.00 g ULV Sodium Alginate (KIMICA ULV-L3/L5 or Protanal LFR 5/60, 32% dry; or 8.25 g NH4-Alg for 90°C fries) for 5 min at room temperature — zero heating/cooking.
  3. Mix in 12.00 g platy kaolin (48%, PVC = 42% < 56% CPVC), then 1.25 g micron TCP (350–650 nm, 5%), and 2.13 g dry eq. (4.25 g of 50%) Ca-Stearate dispersion (8.5%).
  4. Pull ONE #12 Meyer rod pass (~7.8 g/m² dry) on smooth MG kraft/SBS (Rq ~0.9–1.3 µm) and bake 30 s @ 125°C (predicted: 609–674 cP, Cobb60 12.8–16.2, Kit 12.0, Crease Kit 11.9–12.0).

Track B: Low-Cost Production Grade (MILL-6, 30% Solids, 1.55–2.20 ¢/m²)

  • 50–51% Platy Kaolin (Imerys Barrisurf AR ≥ 45) for tortuous oil labyrinth.
  • 19.5% 40 kDa Thin-Boiling / Acid-Modified Cornstarch (21 CFR §182.90) — keeps viscosity at ~653–693 cP while cutting binder cost cleanly below DE-2 dextrin failures.
  • 15.5% ULV Alginate (≥18 vol% of organic phase to maintain contiguous egg-box percolation).
  • 6.5% Ca-Stearate (180° crease slip + 90°C barrier) + 4.0–4.2% Micron TCP (350–650 nm, non-nano) + 3.3–4.5% DAP.
  • Die-Ironed Molded Fiber Option: Spray/dip 18 g/m² onto die-ironed pulp (Rq ≤ 1.8 µm) and cure 20 s @ 180°C (245 cP, Cobb60 14.3, Kit 12.0).
