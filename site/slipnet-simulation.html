200 g Single-Pass Bench Screen (35% Solids, 130 mL DI/Soft Water <=30 ppm CaCO3, <=25°C)

  1. Dissolve 0.28 g SHMP (0.40 wt%) in 130 mL cold DI water (<=25°C) — keeps free [Ca2+] = 0.000 mM.
  2. Blend 20.44 g cooled (<=25°C) low-viscosity starch/dextrin (29.2 wt%), 42.70 g standard platy kaolin (61.0 wt%), and 3.50 g standard Sodium Alginate (5.0 wt%).
  3. Stir in 1.54 g food-grade Diammonium Citrate (2.2 wt%) (critical latent proton donor upon NH3 loss) and adjust to pH 8.8–9.0 with dropwise 28% NH4OH.
  4. Add 1.54 g shelf fine CaCO3/PCC (2.2 wt%, d50 <= 1.2 µm), pull ONE #12 Meyer rod pass on smooth/MG cupstock (Rq <=1.25 µm), bake 60 s at 120–125°C, and check hot-oil/water holdout + warm pH 10 Na2CO3 repulpability.
