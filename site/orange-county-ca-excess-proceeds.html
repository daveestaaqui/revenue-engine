Orange County Recovery Procedure & Verification

When a tax deed auction or sheriff's tax sale closes above the upset bid in Orange County, the clerk deposits excess funds into the statutory court registry. Under Cal. Rev. & Tax Code § 4675, junior lienholders have initial notice priority, after which remaining funds belong to the record titleholder or heirs.

Surplus Docket's automated crawler monitors Orange County filings daily, excludes institutional and bank-owned records, flags estates for heir search, and formats an actionable CSV and Excel feed ready for practitioner due diligence (lien examination remains firm responsibility).
