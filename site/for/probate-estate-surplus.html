Practice Area: Estate Administration & Probate Surplus Recovery

Unclaimed Court Registry Funds for Probate & Heirship Practices

A significant portion of municipal tax deed auctions involve properties held by decedents or unprobated estates. Non-attorney asset recovery entities cannot open estates or file probate petitions. We isolate deceased-owner dockets for licensed probate counsel.

View Subscription Plans Review Verification Protocol
01

Estate Docket Identification

Our indexing rules automatically flag deeds titled to "Estate of", "Heirs of", or individuals with recorded death certificates, isolating matters where formal probate or summary administration is required.

02

Exclusively Attorney-Gated

Clerks will not release estate funds to third-party finders without a certified court order. Only licensed attorneys can petition the probate court for Letters of Administration, creating a protected practice niche.

03

Cross-County Heir Tracking

Our feeds link property situs addresses to county probate filings and official recorded death notices, allowing counsel to verify whether an administration proceeding has already commenced.
