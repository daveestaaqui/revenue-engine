Practice Focus: Judicial Foreclosure Surplus & Lien Priority

Mortgage Foreclosure Excess Proceeds Intelligence

When third-party bidding at a foreclosure auction exceeds the foreclosing plaintiff's final judgment balance, the remaining funds belong to junior lienholders and the record owner. We deliver structured court registry balances to real estate litigators.

View Subscription Plans Technical Methodology
01

Final Judgment Math

Our system audits the Certificate of Disbursements and Certificate of Title against the final judgment amount to isolate the precise net surplus retained in the court registry.

02

Junior Encumbrance Audit

We index secondary mortgages, subordinate home equity lines of credit (HELOCs), and HOA assessment judgments named in the foreclosure complaint to establish lien priority tiers.

03

Statutory Claim Procedures

Judicial foreclosure statutes (such as Fla. Stat. §§ 45.031–45.033) govern disbursement frameworks following the Certificate of Disbursements. We track filing horizons across claimants to help legal teams stay ahead of statutory cutoffs and escheatment.
