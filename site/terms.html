1. Public Records Provenance & Authority

Surplus Docket aggregates, filters, formats, and indexes public court records, overage schedules, and tax deed sale registries pursuant to applicable state freedom of information laws and governing statutes, including:

  • Florida: Fla. Stat. § 197.582 and Chapter 119 Public Records.
  • Texas: Tex. Tax Code § 34.04 and Texas Public Information Act.
  • Georgia: O.C.G.A. § 48-4-5 and Georgia Open Records Act.

Surplus Docket is an independent legal data intelligence provider and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any municipal court clerk or government agency.
