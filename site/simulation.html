Sub-Tie A: Hydrophilic Ester / Ionic Compatibilizer Masterbatch
Provides reversible polar ester bonding to APET or EVOH substrates (>8.0 N/15 mm), a sharp 78°C thermal permeability switch (above the 60.8°C Vicat softening point), and built-in divalent cation (Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺) chelation sites so hard wash water (up to 300 ppm Ca²⁺) never passivates the wicking seam.
Sub-Tie B: Anhydride-Functionalized Polyolefin Anchor Masterbatch
Supplied as a separate pellet masterbatch and dry-blended with Sub-Tie A at the coextruder feed throat. Prevents premature ester/anhydride melt cross-linking during compounding while permanently anchoring the tie layer to the floating PE layer (0.92 g/cm³) so 0% polymer dissolves into plant water.
